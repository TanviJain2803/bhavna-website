The initiative

Funding Alcohol & Tobacco Recovery in Low Income Households using Homeopathy.

Nirjara is an alcohol and tobacco recovery programme built specifically for low-income households — combining homeopathy support, counselling, and daily tracking, supported by Jain religious trusts and CSR.

Goal: help at least 10,000 people in the next 12 months. Phase 1: enrolling 50 participants via the app for recovery.
Fund Nirjara Volunteer with Nirjara WhatsApp us

Is your household help's spouse struggling with alcohol — or would you like to enroll someone in our Phase 1 trial? Message us on WhatsApp.

🏠

Household Help Network Outreach

Household maids' husbands are often affected by alcoholism and nicotine addiction — and the women are determined to change the situation for their families. We empower these households as medicine distributors and advocates for their families, at a subsidised rate funded by religious trusts and CSR.

📲

Digital App Tracking

We believe in tracking efficacy by enabling a digital app that collects real data on how the homeopathy treatment performs — turning each recovery into measurable evidence.

🤝

Strategic NGO Collaboration

We are seeking an inaugural collaboration cohort of NGO partners to jointly raise CSR and government grant funding — and channel it into their established network cohorts.

Digital Support

Tracking Recovery,
Day by Day.

Recovery is a daily decision. As part of the Nirjara initiative, we distribute homeopathy support kits alongside an easy-to-use mobile application.

The app allows individuals to check in daily, track their smoke-free and alcohol-free streaks, and visualize their progress to build lasting, positive momentum away from dependence.

निर्जरा

The philosophy

Why “Nirjara”?

In Jain thought, Nirjara is the deliberate shedding of accumulated karma — the patient, willing release of what binds the soul. Recovery from alcohol and tobacco is exactly that: the courageous letting-go of a dependence, one day at a time. The ninth of the twelve Bhavnas gives this programme its name and its spirit — change that begins from intention, भाव से बदलाव.
