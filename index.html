Nirjara निर्जरा

The shedding of what binds

In Jain thought, Nirjara is the deliberate shedding of accumulated karma. Recovery from alcohol and tobacco is exactly that — the patient, courageous release of a dependence, one day at a time, with a community that refuses to look away.

🪔

Awareness & Outreach

Community camps and school or workplace sessions with local health workers — reducing stigma so people seek help before crisis.

🤝

Recovery Circles

Trained counsellors and peer-support circles — confidential, judgement-free, rooted in nonviolence toward oneself.

🌱

Family Follow-through

Support for spouses, parents and children, with structured follow-ups for a full year after the programme.

~16 crpeople in India consume alcohol; ~5.7 cr affected by harmful use*
~27 crtobacco users — among the highest burdens in the world*
13 lakh+lives lost to tobacco-related illness every year in India*

*Indicative — MoSJE national survey 2019, GATS-2, WHO. Verify before public launch.

Digital Support

Tracking Recovery,
Day by Day.

Recovery is a daily decision. As part of the Nirjara initiative, we distribute homeopathy support kits alongside an easy-to-use mobile application.

The app allows individuals to check in daily, track their smoke-free and alcohol-free streaks, and visualize their progress to build lasting, positive momentum away from dependence.

For Companies & Fellow NGOs

Partner with us.

Nirjara is built as a credible, measurable home for CSR investment in preventive healthcare — and an open platform for NGOs in de-addiction and mental health to collaborate, not compete.

CSR Programme Funding

Adopt Nirjara for a district, a year, or a cohort — with transparent unit costs and quarterly impact reporting.

  • Named cohort or district programmes
  • Quarterly impact & utilisation reports
  • Site visits & employee engagement days

Employee Volunteering

Bring your teams into the work — awareness drives, mentorship, and payroll giving with corporate matching.

  • Structured volunteering calendars
  • Workplace tobacco-cessation drives
  • Matched payroll giving

NGO Collaboration

Share referral pathways, counsellor training, and community networks instead of duplicating them.

  • Referral & follow-up partnerships
  • Shared counsellor training
  • Joint grant applications
CSR eligibility: De-addiction and preventive healthcare align with Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013. Registration, 12A/80G and CSR-1 details will appear here on completion. Partners should verify alignment with their CSR counsel.
